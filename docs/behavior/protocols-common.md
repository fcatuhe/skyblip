## Test files

### [protocols/common/test/test_crc.cpp](../../firmware/protocols/common/test/test_crc.cpp)

The two CRCs the air frames carry: the 24-bit one of ADS-L and Mode S, and CRC-16 CCITT of ALP-TAS.

### [protocols/common/test/test_en300220.cpp](../../firmware/protocols/common/test/test_en300220.cpp)

A logarithm a tenth of a dB off moves the CCA threshold a device declares under EN 300 220-1.

### [protocols/common/test/test_exponential.cpp](../../firmware/protocols/common/test/test_exponential.cpp)

The exponential codes ADS-L G.1.6 packs its fields into, which the ALP-TAS frame shares with other widths: a code that rounds the wrong way reaches the pilot as a target at the wrong height or speed.

### [protocols/common/test/test_geometry.cpp](../../firmware/protocols/common/test/test_geometry.cpp)

The integer geometry the stacks place a target with: a sine a quadrant out or a root that rounds down puts a target in the wrong place, with nothing logged.

### [protocols/common/test/test_manchester.cpp](../../firmware/protocols/common/test/test_manchester.cpp)

Manchester coding checked in both directions: a decoder that hides a flipped chip instead of flagging it corrupts traffic the CRC then has to catch by luck.

### [protocols/common/test/test_xxtea.cpp](../../firmware/protocols/common/test/test_xxtea.cpp)

The XXTEA scrambler ADS-L and ALP-TAS share: a scrambler that is not its own inverse corrupts every frame, and the avalanche case keeps a long run of identical bits off the air.
