### [protocols/adsl/test/test_media_access.cpp](../../firmware/protocols/adsl/test/test_media_access.cpp)

§D.3's media access, one branch a case: what a busy channel does to a packet waiting to go out.

### [protocols/adsl/test/test_packet.cpp](../../firmware/protocols/adsl/test/test_packet.cpp)

The ADS-L position packet end to end, a radio protocol checked with no RF hardware in the room: the channel is an injectable bit error rate, and the packet has to survive it or say that it did not. What these separate is a *detected failure*, a frame dropped and counted, from a *silent miscorrection*, a frame that decodes to a plausible aircraft somewhere it is not. Only the second one collides with anybody.

The transmit rules ADS-L sets: G.1.16's rate, the two M-band channels and §C.5's slots.

### [protocols/adsl/test/test_uplink.cpp](../../firmware/protocols/adsl/test/test_uplink.cpp)

The ADS-L uplink air<->ground round-trip, pure host, no hardware. Proves air+ground share one codec. Asserts RS(255,223) corrects all patterns up to 16 symbol errors and, beyond 16, that silent miscorrection is counted SEPARATELY from detected failure.
