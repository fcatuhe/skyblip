# Power run: skyBlip Go E68BD9 as a Tango, 2 Hz GNSS, discharge to cutoff, release 0.8.0

The same plain T-Echo, parked, paired with a Bravo and connected to it over Bluetooth for the whole run, its L76K at 2 Hz, ran from 4155 mV at 97 % to the cutoff at 3192 mV in 19 h 00 min. That is 9 min longer than its run alone at 1 Hz on build 987 (`2026-10-14-power-run-E68BD9.md`). The second run's projection gave the Bravo link and the 2 Hz receiver about 2.5 mA together and this run about 17 h 50 min. The total draw did not rise, and the two runs' curves match within two points at every voltage, but the build changed too, so this run does not price the two extras. Release 0.8.0 adds a Status burst every 10 s and keys 50 % more air time, which costs under 0.1 mA. The duty records cannot see the link to the Bravo, because `ble_connected_ms` counts only the centrals connected to the Go.

The pilot sent the capture from the device page at 26 Nov 12:07, 49 min after the cutoff, and the server keeps it as capture 1. The unit read `charging` true then. The capture had drained to flash, so it outlived the cutoff and the wake.

## The discharge

`scripts/power_budget.py --pack-mah 850`:

| | mA | |
|---|---|---|
| modelled | 39.9 | GNSS 29.0, receive 4.7, nRF52840 3.5, TCXO 2.0, the rest under 1 |
| measured | 43.4 | 824 mAh, 97 points of the gauge, over 19 h 00 min |
| residual | -3.5 | the table explains 92 % of what the cell lost |

On the 850 mAh rating alone, from the first reading to the cutoff, the three runs of this unit drew 46.0, 45.1 and 44.7 mA, and each lasted longer than the one before: 18 h 29 min, 18 h 51 min and 19 h 00 min. The model integrates to 758 mAh, against 749 and 735 on the earlier runs.

The model has no row for the 2 Hz receiver or for the Go's link to its Bravo, so its 39.9 mA describes a Go alone at 1 Hz. The measured figure includes both.

### What the Tango and the 2 Hz receiver cost

Run 2's In flight, projected section estimated the extras over a parked Go alone at 1 Hz: 1.5 mA for a Bravo connected over Bluetooth and 1.0 mA (0.5 to 1.5) for the L76K at 2 Hz. On run 2's 824 mAh, 2.5 mA more would have ended this run near 17 h 50 min. It ended at 19 h 00 min, on the same charge from the gauge's curve, 824.5 mAh.

So a Go as a Tango at 2 Hz on 0.8.0 draws no more than a Go alone at 1 Hz on build 987, within the spread between runs of this unit, about 2 %, or 1 mA. That bounds the total, not the extras: 0.8.0 is about 1400 builds past 987 and may draw less elsewhere, enough to offset them. The cooler room works the other way: this run's die sat 4 C below run 2's, and a colder cell gives less charge. A Go alone at 2 Hz on 0.8.0 would split the two.

Transmit: b23b8818 sends a Status every ten seconds on the other channel, three more 5 ms bursts every 30 s. The whole transmit row costs 0.13 mA. Parked keying is now about 90 ms a minute against the 160 that `power_budget.py` reads as airborne, so the posture still reads right.

Panel: the refreshes follow the time of day, about 300 an hour in the afternoon and the morning and none from 23:18 to 03:18 UTC. That matches the traffic a Bravo hears, airliners on ADS-B by day, but a power run records no traffic, so nothing in the capture confirms it. At 460 ms of settle each, the model charges them 0.15 mA.

Bluetooth: the 850 s are the briefing app's link that armed the capture, held 14 min. `ble_connected_ms` is `ConfigLinkService`'s on-time, the time at least one central was connected to the Go (`core/bus/README.md`). The Go is the central of its link to the Bravo (`products/skyblip_go/services/bravo.cpp`), so 19 h of that link appear nowhere in the duty records.

## In flight, projected

A Tango in flight sends a position every second instead of every ten. At this run's 0.0014 mA per millisecond keyed a minute, about 270 ms more a minute adds about 0.4 mA. On this run's 824 mAh that is about 18 h 50 min to the cutoff, with the Bravo link and the 2 Hz receiver included, as measured here. The projection leaves out what flight adds to the glass and the alarms, as run 2's did.
