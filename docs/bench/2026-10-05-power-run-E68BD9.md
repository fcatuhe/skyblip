# Power run: skyBlip Go E68BD9, charge then discharge to cutoff, build 942

The first discharge to cutoff on silicon: a plain T-Echo, parked, went from 4158 mV just off the cable to the cutoff at 3197 mV in 18 h 29 min. On its 850 mAh rating that is 45 mA on average, against 40 mA modelled. The run also gives the first measured discharge curve. The textbook curve reads up to 15 points high between 3.70 and 3.80 V, and the battery level flips back and forth for minutes at each threshold.

## The charge: the divider reads the USB rail

From 06:24:19 to 09:58:49 on the cable, the 430 `power` records all read 4670 to 4700 mV, with `external_power` true and `charging` false, while the die rose to 39.7 C. A cell in constant current from 45 % reads well under 4.2 V. Values pinned at 4.7 V are the rail the USB lifts the divider to, not the cell. 4700 is also the top of the window `zephyr::Battery::read_mv` accepts. 0B1B2C on its charger read above it, so every reading was refused (#114). On this unit VBUS detect does work: the cable was seen.

The charge took about 3.6 hours, from the 45 % the unit was switched off at the evening before. No charge curve can come from the divider on either unit.

## The discharge

`scripts/power_budget.py --pack-mah 850 --session 1791095030`:

| | mA | |
|---|---|---|
| modelled | 40.3 | GNSS 29.0, receive 4.7, nRF52840 3.5, TCXO 2.0, the rest under 1 |
| measured | 45.1 | 833 mAh, 98 points of the gauge, over 18 h 29 min |
| residual | -4.7 | the table explains 89 % of what the cell lost |

The run is not quite whole: its first reading off the cable is 98 %, under the 99 % the script requires. The measured figure assumes the 850 mAh rating: a pack below its rating draws less than 45 mA for the same hours. The model includes the 0.6 mA `IMU hub` row on a unit with no IMU, because `power_budget.py` does not read `capabilities`.

The cell sat on a plateau between 3800 and 3750 mV for five hours, a quarter of the run, where the textbook spends 15 points. Below it, the textbook stays up to 15 points high until the knee. The ladder holds up: `kLowMv` (3600) came 1.8 h before the cutoff and `kCriticalMv` (3500) 47 min before it. From 3400 mV the end is quick: 20 minutes to the cutoff.

### The levels flicker at each threshold

The level crossed back and forth five times between `normal` and `low` from 02:39:49 to 02:44:49, at 3591 to 3601 mV, and three times between `low` and `critical` within a minute at 3492 to 3501 mV. Each change moves the lamp, the radar ring and the status row. Three consecutive samples on the other side of a threshold (`kLevelSamples`) do not stop it. Inside a 30-minute window the reading spreads 35 mV at the median, which is wider than the band the cell takes minutes to cross, and `core/power/README.md` says the three-sample rule prevents exactly this.

## Against 0B1B2C

Over the same voltages, the T-Echo Plus (`2026-10-05-power-run-0B1B2C.md`) took 3.1 to 4.8 times as long as this unit, while its rated pack is only 2.8 times larger. Either the Plus draws less, around 30 mA against 45, or its pack holds more than 2400 mAh, or this pack holds less than 850. The two cells may also have different shapes. 0B1B2C's run to cutoff will measure its pack and settle it.
